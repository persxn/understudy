    A book is a box; open, it shows its chapters, and a chapter lists its sections. Arrows point from a chapter to the chapter that needs it; an arrow is left out when a longer route already says the same. Tab into the graph, then: Right to what a chapter unlocks, Left to what it needs, Up and Down to the box above or below, Enter to select, open or fold, + and − to zoom, 0 to fit, Esc back to the tree. A heavy red solid border marks what the selection needs first, a blue dashed border what it unlocks, yellow the selection. Drag or scroll to pan; pinch, or Ctrl and scroll, to zoom.

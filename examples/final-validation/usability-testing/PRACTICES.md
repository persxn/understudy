# Usability testing practices worth teaching

Sources read 2026-11-18 as reading material only:
- [Figma share] Figma Learn, "Share or embed files and prototypes", https://help.figma.com/hc/en-us/articles/360040531773 (prototype links, access, plan limits)
- [Figma flows] Figma Learn, "Create and manage prototype flows", https://help.figma.com/hc/en-us/articles/360039823894 (flow starting points), read 2026-11-18 in review of chapter 1

## Habits

1. Write the question the test must answer before writing any task. From chapter 1.
2. Get the participant's consent before starting, and before any recording, in words they can refuse without awkwardness. From chapter 1.
3. Say "we are testing the design, not you" at the start of every session. From chapter 1.
4. Write notes as what was seen or said, with the time, apart from your interpretation. From chapter 1.
5. Never name the button, label or screen the participant is looking for in a task. From chapter 1 (taught fully in chapter 3).
6. Answer a participant's question with a question ("what would you expect?") rather than the answer. From chapter 1.
7. Run a pilot session before real participants. From chapter 3.
8. Rate every issue's severity before deciding what goes in the report. From chapter 5.
9. Report the top issues first, each with evidence (a quote or clip) and a recommendation. From chapter 6.

## Facts that change

- Figma prototypes exist only in Figma Design files. [Figma share, 2026-11-18]
- A prototype-only link is made from presentation view (Present, then Share prototype) and shows the prototype without the design file. [Figma share, 2026-11-18]
- Prototype-only links need a paid Figma plan; on the free Starter plan a viewer of a shared prototype can reach the source file through "Open in editor". [Figma share, 2026-11-18]
- A prototype plays from a flow starting point, set on a top-level frame in the Prototype tab (one per frame; added automatically when two unconnected frames are first linked); a shared prototype lets viewers choose another flow. [Figma flows, 2026-11-18]
- Password protection is for paid plans and link expiry for the Enterprise plan only. [Figma share, 2026-11-18]

## Handle with care

- Recording a session (screen, face or voice): only with the participant's spoken or written consent for that recording; say who will see it and when it is deleted; the book stores recordings away from shared folders and quotes people by participant code, never by name.
- Sharing a prototype link with participants on a free Figma plan: they may open the whole design file; the book duplicates the prototype into a file holding nothing else before sharing.
- Testing a company's unreleased prototype: check what you may show outside the company before recruiting friends or classmates; the book uses the reader's own prototype for practice.
- Paying or rewarding participants: agree the amount and form with the team first; the book never promises a reward the reader cannot give.

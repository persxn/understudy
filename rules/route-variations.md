# Route variations

Read when a route is not the plain case.

- **Deadline:** fit the route to the date and hours; say plainly which level is reachable.
- **Re-plan:** rebuild from the current goal; keep everything proven, and keep chapters marked `skip`.
- **Two goals:** a chapter both need appears once; weave both into one weekly order.
- **Their own course, book or playlist:** adopt chapters they already have with `python3 scripts/us_plan.py from-chapters <book>`, or map the course's parts onto chapters of a plan by title, and fill only the gaps.
- **Security goal:** read `rules/track-security.md` first; its limits and shape are fixed.
- **Algorithms and contests:** plan the book here; the training loop belongs to `us-compete`.
- **Engineer standard:** if it applies, add its long-running items beside the route, not inside every chapter. See `rules/engineer-standard.md`.
- **Shelves:** when a chapter has a paper, case study, outage story or practice site worth the learner's time, add a short file under `book/library/` (link, date checked, why it matters, what to read first, which chapters it belongs with) and point to it from the route. Link and guide; never copy.
